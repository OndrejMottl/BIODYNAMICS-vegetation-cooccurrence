## Findings to carry into Stage B

- **Spatial stores were already outdated before Stage A.** `fit_sjsdm_cross_validation_candidate()`, `prepare_sjsdm_tuning_folds()` and `compute_spatial_mev_basis()` changed in #178 (23 October 2026), after the spatial stores were built (9-10 October). `{targets}` therefore marks every cross-validation work item, the final fits and the partitions as outdated in every spatial store (`baseline_store_freshness.csv`). On a copy of `eu_r002_l001`, rebuilding the MEM basis and the prepared folds left the folds and the MEMs identical; only the runtime field `elapsed_seconds` of the MEM provenance changed. The #178 change to the CV fitter touches only the failed-fit path. A full `tar_make()` on any spatial store would nevertheless refit everything. Selective reruns would therefore need `shortcut = TRUE` and a ledger row for this drift; the full rebuild from the corrected VegVault release, now planned for Stage B, makes the drift irrelevant.
- **Partition targets are masked by that drift.** In the audit, European stores show no blocking target beyond the baseline because their partition targets were already outdated through the #178 cascade.
- **`bio4` (temperature seasonality) is 0 for the most recent samples and for most modern plots.** In the VegVault values extracted for the paleo temporal stores, `bio4` is 0 for 97-100 % of fossil samples aged 250 cal yr BP or younger, and never 0 for older samples (median about 600-900); the other six climate variables of the same samples are plausible. A value of 0 would mean identical mean temperature in every month, which does not occur in these regions, so the zeros are a missing value in the most recent climate layer, not a real value. Consequences: (1) at 0 ka every interpolated `bio4` value is 0 (Europe 26, America 22, Asia 1 core), so the predictor is constant, scaling divides by a zero standard deviation and sjSDM fails with `ZeroDivisionError` (the 0 ka failure of issue #181); (2) interpolation also mixes the zeros into later slices (Europe 28 of 467 values at 500 cal yr BP and 7 more at 1000-2000; America 36 of 309 at 500 and 8 more); (3) the continental fossil-pollen spatial models include the 0 ka samples, whose scaled `bio4` lies 3.5-4.8 standard deviations below all others; (4) in the modern-plot stores `bio4` is 0 for 95 % of European and 92 % of Asian values, and it is a predictor of the European and Asian modern models, where it acts as a near-binary indicator. American modern models do not use `bio4`. **Source (read-only queries on `Data/Input/VegVault.sqlite`, VegVault 1.0.0):** the zeros are stored in VegVault. At age 0, `bio4` is 0 for 99,503 of 106,152 gridpoints (94 %), every one of them south of 65.6 degrees N; the 6,649 non-zero values all lie north of 65.8 degrees N and agree with the same gridpoints at 500 cal yr BP (median ratio 0.99). No other age (500-18,000 cal yr BP) has a zero `bio4`, and no other variable has comparable zeros at age 0. The cached CHELSA-TraCE21k present-day `bio4` raster for Europe (`Data/Temp/chelsa/project_europe/`) has no zeros, so the climate source is not at fault. VegVault's age-0 values appear to come from the modern CHELSA climatology rather than CHELSA-TraCE21k (values rounded to 0.1, `bio12` clipped at the 16-bit maximum of 6553.5 mm at 138 very wet gridpoints, and larger differences from the CHELSA-TraCE21k present-day layer than at 500 cal yr BP); this is an inference, not checked against the CHELSA files. The processed raster `Data/Processed/Neoclimate/CHELSA_bio_04_1981-2010_V2.1.tif` in `VegVault-abiotic_data` already holds the zeros south of 65.7 degrees N; reported as [VegVault#78](https://github.com/OndrejMottl/VegVault/issues/78).
- **The association penalty changes the published variance partitions, always in the same direction.** The pilot rebuilt `list_jsdm_variance_partition_<resolution>` on store copies for six penalised models (`lambda_cov = 0.1`) and one unpenalised control (`variance_partition_penalty_pilot.csv`). In every penalised model the Associations share fell once the sub-models carried the full model's penalty and seed: by 1.2 percentage points (fossil Asia continental, functional types), 3.4 and 9.8 (fossil `eu_r002_l001` family and genus), 11.1 (fossil `eu_r008_l003` family), 14.3 (fossil regional `eu_r004` genus) and 20.6 (modern `eu_r005_l002` family). The freed share went mostly to Abiotic, or to Spatial in the `eu_r002_l001` genus model. The unpenalised control moved by at most 0.3 points, which is the effect of the seed alone. Published association shares of penalised models are therefore overstated, and every penalised partition needs rebuilding. The raw three-way overlap (Abiotic&Associations&Spatial) was already negative in the published partitions and becomes two to three times more negative on the deviance scale. On the Nagelkerke scale this shows as -27 to -691 in `eu_r004` genus, because R^2 = 1 - exp(-D/N) grows exponentially for negative deviance D. The shares clamp negative fractions to zero before the Shapley allocation, so this does not enter them directly. It is still worth describing in the methods. The pilot partitions took 1.15-1.25 times their recorded production runtime (the GPU was shared with the CZ smoke run for part of the pilot).
- **The "no package called 'qs'" errors mean a target was never built, not a cache-format problem.** `{targets}` 1.12.0 reads targets of format `qs` with `qs2`. When that read fails for any reason, including a missing object file, it falls back to the legacy `qs` package, which is not installed, and reports that instead. Every American and Asian store records its targets as format `qs`, and about 34,000 of them read normally. In 255 American and Asian units (modern local 138, fossil local 108, modern regional 8, fossil regional 1), every target downstream of the first failure has no object file. The first failure was VegVault extraction in 239 units ("empty/opaque error message"), constant predictors in 10 and too few cores in 6, all built 4-8 September 2026. The extraction failures are empty windows: read-only queries of `Data/Input/VegVault.sqlite` find no dataset of the unit's type (fossil-pollen archive, or vegetation plot) inside any of the 239 windows within its age range. Every one of the 185 units built in the same tiers holds at least one (median 3 to 733 datasets, depending on tier). The same message also appears as the stored error of those downstream targets, because building them tried to load the missing dependency. `mem_projection_r2_by_unit.csv` labels these units `not_built`. None of them holds a published model.

## Deviations from the implementation plan

- **Configured slice exclusions.** An in-window infeasible slice stops the whole temporal `tar_make()` at its dynamic branches ("cannot branch over empty target"), so per-slice classification alone cannot keep the other slices running. Profiles therefore accept `temporal_slices.excluded_ages`; Asia excludes 0 ka (one sample). Excluded slices appear in the runner status file and in Stage 04 as `expected_infeasible` with reason `excluded_in_configuration`, so an exclusion is never silent. The change is declared in `configuration_reference_changes.csv`.
- **Predictor guard in the formula builder.** `build_jsdm_environment_formula()` aborts with class `jsdm_formula_predictor_not_finite` when a numeric predictor has no finite value, instead of the opaque sjSDM `ZeroDivisionError`. It adds no local, and the serialized formula and its `{targets}` hash are identical to `main` for every resolution of `eu_r002_l001`, the European continental store, and `am_r011_l004`, with and without age terms. Europe and America 0 ka now stop with this error until the `bio4` input is resolved.
- **Additional cascade rules.** The temporal cascade rules in `classify_sjsdm_unit_pipeline_error()` follow the 28 errors recorded when Asia 0 ka was built on a store copy; the same cascade without its insufficient-sample root stays `unexpected_error` (tested).
- **Fail-closed temporal runners.** Fit runners 07-09 stop when `run_pipeline()` raises an unexplained failure, when any slice is `unexpected_error`, or when a slice has no built `list_jsdm_variance_partition_<slice>` (`not_built`).
- **Clear message for empty VegVault windows.** `build_vegvault_plan()` now aborts with class `biodynamics_vegvault_no_records` and names the dataset type and window when the plan holds no non-gridpoint sample. Before, gridpoints kept such a plan non-empty and `vaultkeepr::get_abiotic_data(verbose = FALSE)` then stopped with an empty message ("empty/opaque error message"). The classifier already treated both messages as `no_spatial_records`. The edit outdates `data_vegvault_extracted` (and the paleo age-uncertainty extraction) in every store; rebuilding it reads the same data, so `{targets}` skips the downstream targets.
- **Missing stores.** The store validator treats a missing temporal, tier-tuning or sensitivity store, or a store named with `--store`, as blocking; missing spatial unit stores stay informational because most grid units have none.
