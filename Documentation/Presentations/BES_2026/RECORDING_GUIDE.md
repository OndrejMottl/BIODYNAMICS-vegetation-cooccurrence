# BES 2026 recording guide

Checklist for recording and uploading the 15-minute online talk for the BES Annual Meeting 2026. Upload deadline on Whova: **Wednesday 25 November 2026, 17:00 GMT (18:00 CET)**.

## Before recording

- Render the deck (`Documentation/Presentations/BES_2026/R/render.R`) and open `_output/index.html` in Chrome or Edge.
- Press `S` to open the speaker view with the narration on the second screen (allow pop-ups), and `F` for full screen on the shared screen.
- Click through once: every fragment appears on an arrow key or space, and the animated GIFs (slides 08, 11, 12) play.
- Turn off notifications, close other applications, and record in a quiet room; carpet helps.
- Put the camera at or just above eye level, face the light (a window or a lamp), not with it behind you.
- Do a 30-second test recording and listen back.

## Recording with Zoom

1. Start a Zoom meeting with video on (*Host a Meeting*, *With Video On*).
2. Share the browser window that shows the deck, not the whole desktop.
3. Press *Record*, choose *Record on this Computer*, and keep the camera on, so viewers see your face in the corner.
4. Read the speaker notes at a calm pace. BES asks presenters to speak slower than in person.
5. Advance each fragment when the narration reaches it; let the GIFs run for a moment on slides 08, 11 and 12. Slides after the final slide are extras for questions and are not recorded.
6. Stop the recording after the final slide. Zoom converts it to an MP4 file when the meeting ends.
7. Watch the whole recording before uploading. BES requires this.

Target length: at most 15 minutes. `script.md` estimates 12:37 at 130 words per minute, which leaves room for pauses and for the animations to run.

## Uploading to YouTube

1. Upload the MP4 in YouTube Studio.
2. Visibility: **Unlisted**, not Private. Whova viewers must be able to play it without being invited.
3. Title: *Exploring biotic signals in vegetation assembly from the LGM to the Anthropocene (BES 2026)*.
4. Description: use the text below and adjust the chapter times to the recording.
5. Captions: *Subtitles* > *Add language* (English) > *Add* > *Auto-sync*, paste the contents of `transcript.txt`, and check the result. BES encourages captions.

How much of the structure of Northern Hemisphere vegetation since the Last Glacial Maximum remains after climate and spatial structure, and does that residual association depend on spatial scale, taxonomic resolution and time?
