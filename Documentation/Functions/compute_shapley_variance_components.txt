Compute Shapley-Allocated Variance Components

Description:

     Converts a long-format variance-partitioning table into per-age
     percentages for Abiotic, Associations, and Spatial components
     using equal-split Shapley allocation.

Usage:

     compute_shapley_variance_components(data_source)

Arguments:

data_source: A non-empty data frame with columns `age`, `component`,
          and `R2_Nagelkerke`. Missing intersection labels are treated
          as zero, and negative R² values are clamped to zero.

Details:

     Each two-way intersection is split equally between its two
     components, and the three-way intersection is split equally among
     all three components. The adjusted values therefore sum to the
     total of all seven clamped fractions.

Value:

     A tibble with exactly three rows per age and columns `age`,
     `component`, `R2_Nagelkerke_adjusted`, and
     `R2_Nagelkerke_percentage`.

See Also:

     [extract_jsdm_variance_fractions()],
     [aggregate_jsdm_variance_components()]
