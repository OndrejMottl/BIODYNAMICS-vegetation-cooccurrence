Aggregate sjSDM Tuning Fit Attempts

Description:

     Extracts and combines candidate attempt provenance from the
     compact tuning prediction cache.

Usage:

     aggregate_sjsdm_tuning_fit_attempts(list_prediction_cache = NULL)

Arguments:

list_prediction_cache: Fold-organized prediction cache from
          [aggregate_sjsdm_tuning_work_items()].

Value:

     Attempt-level tibble ordered by repeat, fold, candidate, and
     attempt.

Examples:

     aggregate_sjsdm_tuning_fit_attempts(base::list())
