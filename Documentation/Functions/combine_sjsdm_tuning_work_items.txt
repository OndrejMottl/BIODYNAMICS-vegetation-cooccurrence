Combine sjSDM Tuning Work Items

Description:

     Combines granular candidate/fold results into the established
     tuning table and fold-organized compact prediction cache.

Usage:

     combine_sjsdm_tuning_work_items(list_work_item_results = NULL)

Arguments:

list_work_item_results: List of results from
          [run_sjsdm_tuning_work_item()].

Value:

     Named list with `data_tuning` and `list_prediction_cache`,
     matching the rich return contract of
     [run_sjsdm_tuning_candidates()].
