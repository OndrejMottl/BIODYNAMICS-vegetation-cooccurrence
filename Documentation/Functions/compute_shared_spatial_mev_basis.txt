Compute the Shared Spatial MEM Basis

Description:

     Applies the common spatial-predictor configuration and constructs
     the reusable 2-D MEM basis when spatial predictors are enabled.

Usage:

     compute_shared_spatial_mev_basis(
       data_coords_projected,
       config_spatial_predictors,
       compute_basis_function = compute_spatial_mev_basis
     )

Arguments:

data_coords_projected: Projected input coordinates.

config_spatial_predictors: Shared spatial-predictor configuration.

compute_basis_function: Basis constructor. Defaults to
          [compute_spatial_mev_basis()].

Value:

     Reusable spatial MEM basis list, or `NULL` when 2-D spatial
     predictors are disabled.
