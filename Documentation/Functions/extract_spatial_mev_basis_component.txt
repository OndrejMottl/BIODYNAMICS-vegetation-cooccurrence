Extract an Optional Spatial MEM Basis Component

Description:

     Extracts one explicit component from a reusable spatial MEM basis
     while preserving `NULL` when the basis is disabled.

Usage:

     extract_spatial_mev_basis_component(list_spatial_mev_basis, component_name)

Arguments:

list_spatial_mev_basis: Reusable spatial MEM basis list or `NULL`.

component_name: Character scalar naming the component to extract.

Value:

     Selected component or `NULL`.
