Summarise Predictive Model Metrics

Description:

     Averages successful community-level predictive metrics across
     repeats.

Usage:

     summarise_predictive_model_metrics(model_evaluation_cross_validated)

Arguments:

model_evaluation_cross_validated: Cross-validated model-evaluation
          object, or `NULL`.

Value:

     A one-row tibble containing Tjur R2, AUC, and log-loss summaries.
