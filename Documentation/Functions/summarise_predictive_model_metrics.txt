Summarise Predictive Model Metrics

Description:

     Averages successful community-level predictive metrics across
     repeats.

Usage:

     summarise_predictive_model_metrics(list_pooled_cv_evaluation)

Arguments:

list_pooled_cv_evaluation: Cross-validated model-evaluation object, or
          `NULL`.

Value:

     A one-row tibble containing Tjur R2, AUC, and log-loss summaries.
