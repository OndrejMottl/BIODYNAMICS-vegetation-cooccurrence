Assess the Paired sjSDM Staged Benchmark

Description:

     Compares paired exhaustive and staged benchmark repetitions
     against the frozen issue 138 runtime, resource, technical, and
     scientific gates.

Usage:

     evaluate_sjsdm_staged_benchmark(
       data_benchmark_runs = NULL,
       list_policy = build_sjsdm_staged_benchmark_policy()
     )

Arguments:

data_benchmark_runs: One row per strategy and benchmark repetition
          containing measured resources, fit counts, predictive
          metrics, technical hashes, and selection evidence.

list_policy: Named list containing the versioned issue 138 acceptance
          thresholds. Defaults to
          [build_sjsdm_staged_benchmark_policy()].

Value:

     A named list containing paired comparisons, criterion-level gate
     results, and a one-row benchmark decision.
