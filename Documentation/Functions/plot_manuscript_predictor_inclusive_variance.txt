Plot Manuscript Predictor-Inclusive Variance

Description:

     Builds the shared manuscript figure for overlapping
     predictor-inclusive variance contributions across spatial tiers
     and taxonomic resolutions.

Usage:

     plot_manuscript_predictor_inclusive_variance(data_plot, data_summary)

Arguments:

data_plot: Prepared unit-level predictor-inclusive variance data.

data_summary: Grouped median and empirical 95 percent interval data.

Value:

     A `ggplot` object with predictor domains in rows and resolutions
     in columns.
