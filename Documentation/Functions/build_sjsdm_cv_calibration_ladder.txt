Build the sjSDM CV Calibration Budget Ladder

Description:

     Constructs the fixed iteration ladder at the configured screening
     sampling followed by the maximum-iteration sampling ladder used
     only when iteration calibration fails.

Usage:

     build_sjsdm_cv_calibration_ladder(iteration_sampling = 100L)

Arguments:

iteration_sampling: Positive integer sampling budget used for the
          iteration-screening rungs.

Value:

     Tibble with deterministic budget order, phase, iterations, and
     sampling.

Examples:

     build_sjsdm_cv_calibration_ladder()
