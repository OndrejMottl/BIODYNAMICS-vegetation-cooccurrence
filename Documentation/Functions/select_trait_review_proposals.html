Select Trait Review Proposals

Description

Selects one proposal for every trait-review candidate from the automated sources, in a fixed order of precedence: the conservative policy, then the interim closure, then the human review queue.

Usage

select_trait_review_proposals(
  data_trait_review_candidates,
  data_policy_proposals,
  data_closure_decisions,
  data_queue_suggestions
)

Arguments

data_trait_review_candidates

Current review candidates with unique 'candidate_id', 'taxon_name', and 'trait_domain_name'.

data_policy_proposals

Policy proposals in the canonical decision columns, at most one row per candidate (from [build_trait_review_policy_outputs()]).

data_closure_decisions

Closure decisions in the canonical decision columns, at most one row per candidate (from [build_trait_review_interim_closure()]).

data_queue_suggestions

Suggested decisions in the canonical decision columns for candidates that need human review, for example scale proposals of [build_trait_review_programmatic_reconciliation()]. A candidate may have several rows.

Details

A candidate with a policy proposal takes it, and its closure decision and suggestions are not used. A candidate without one takes its closure decision. All other candidates form the queue. Nothing is approved here: 'has_overridden_suggestion' marks policy and closure proposals of candidates that also have a queue suggestion, so the reviewer can see which suggestions were set aside.

Value

A tibble in the canonical decision columns plus 'proposal_source' ('"policy"', '"closure"', or '"queue"') and 'has_overridden_suggestion'. Every row has 'review_status' '"proposed"' and no reviewer or review date. Queue candidates keep all their suggestion rows; a queue candidate without a suggestion gets one row with no 'decision_id' and no 'action', to be completed by the reviewer. Rows follow the candidate order.
