Assess a Paired Spatial MEM Benchmark

Description:

     Compares exact and fast spatial MEM runs on identical repetitions
     against the Issue 138 technical and predictive regression
     allowances.

Usage:

     assess_spatial_mev_paired_benchmark(
       data_benchmark_runs = NULL,
       list_policy = get_sjsdm_staged_benchmark_policy()
     )

Arguments:

data_benchmark_runs: One row per repetition and spatial MEM strategy.

list_policy: Issue 138 benchmark policy. Defaults to
          [get_sjsdm_staged_benchmark_policy()].

Value:

     Named list with paired comparisons, gate results, and one decision
     row.
