Summarise Fitted AUC

Description:

     Summarises finite species-level AUC values from fitted-model
     evaluation.

Usage:

     .summarise_fitted_auc(list_jsdm_evaluation_fitted)

Arguments:

list_jsdm_evaluation_fitted: Fitted-model evaluation object, or `NULL`.

Value:

     A one-row tibble containing fitted AUC summary statistics.
