Convert a v1 sjSDM Tier Regularization Selection

Description:

     Validates the frozen v1 tier-selection table and upgrades its
     embedded schema marker without changing scientific content or
     creation provenance.

Usage:

     convert_v1_sjsdm_tier_regularization_selection(data_selection = NULL)

Arguments:

data_selection: Frozen v1 tier-selection table.

Value:

     Exact v2 tier-selection table.
