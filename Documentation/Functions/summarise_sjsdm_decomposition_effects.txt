Summarize Matched-Fold sjSDM Decomposition Effects

Description:

     Aggregates paired component-removal effects using taxon means
     within folds and fold means within repeats for all and eligible
     taxa.

Usage:

     summarise_sjsdm_decomposition_effects(data_comparisons = NULL)

Arguments:

data_comparisons: Output from
          [compute_sjsdm_decomposition_fold_effects()].

Details:

     Only pairs with status `"ok"` and finite raw effects are
     summarized. Negative component effects remain negative at every
     aggregation level.

Value:

     A named list containing fold effects, repeat effects, and
     descriptive repeat summaries with 95 percent quantile intervals.

Examples:

     ## Not run:

     summarise_sjsdm_decomposition_effects(data_comparisons)
     ## End(Not run)
