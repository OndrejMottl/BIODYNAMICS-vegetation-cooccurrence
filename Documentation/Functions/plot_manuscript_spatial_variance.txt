Plot Manuscript Spatial Variance Partitioning

Description:

     Builds the shared two-panel variance-partitioning figure used for
     paleo and modern results: normalized component composition and
     unit-level association spread with median and 95 percent interval.

Usage:

     plot_manuscript_spatial_variance(
       data_plot,
       data_component_summary,
       data_biotic_summary
     )

Arguments:

data_plot: Prepared unit-level spatial variance data.

data_component_summary: Grouped component-composition data.

data_biotic_summary: Grouped association summary data.

Value:

     A `cowplot` assembled plot object.
