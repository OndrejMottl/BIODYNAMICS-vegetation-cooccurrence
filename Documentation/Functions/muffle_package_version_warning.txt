Muffle Package-Version Warnings

Description:

     Suppresses package build-version warnings replayed by future
     workers.

Usage:

     .muffle_package_version_warning(condition_captured)

Arguments:

condition_captured: A warning condition passed by
          [base::withCallingHandlers()].

Value:

     `NULL`, invisibly. Matching package-version warnings are muffled
     through the active warning restart.
