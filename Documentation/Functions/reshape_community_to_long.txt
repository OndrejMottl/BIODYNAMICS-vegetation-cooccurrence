Reshape Community Data to Long Format

Description:

     Reshapes wide community counts to one taxon observation per row.

Usage:

     reshape_community_to_long(data_community = NULL)

Arguments:

data_community: A wide data frame containing `dataset_name`,
          `sample_name`, and at least one taxon-count column.

Details:

     Missing taxon counts are omitted from the returned long data.

Value:

     A data frame in long format with columns `dataset_name`,
     `sample_name`, `taxon`, and `pollen_count`.

Examples:

     data_community <-
       tibble::tibble(
         dataset_name = "example",
         sample_name = "sample_1",
         Pinus = 10,
         Betula = 5
       )

     reshape_community_to_long(data_community = data_community)
