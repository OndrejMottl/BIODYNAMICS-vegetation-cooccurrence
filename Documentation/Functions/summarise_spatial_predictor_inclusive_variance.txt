Summarise Spatial Predictor-Inclusive Variance

Description:

     Summarises unit-level predictor-inclusive percentages for spatial
     variance figures using medians and empirical 95 percent intervals.

Usage:

     summarise_spatial_predictor_inclusive_variance(data_plot)

Arguments:

data_plot: Data frame returned by
          [prepare_spatial_variance_plot_data()].

Value:

     A tibble with one row per scale, resolution, and predictor domain,
     including `n_units`, `median`, `lwr_95`, and `upr_95`.
