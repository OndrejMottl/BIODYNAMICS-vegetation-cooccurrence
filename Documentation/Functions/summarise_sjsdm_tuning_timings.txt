Collect sjSDM Tuning Stage Timings

Description:

     Converts compact fold prediction caches into a long timing table
     for fold preparation, fitting, prediction, and scoring stages.

Usage:

     summarise_sjsdm_tuning_timings(list_prediction_cache = NULL)

Arguments:

list_prediction_cache: Fold caches returned by
          [run_sjsdm_tuning_candidates()] when prediction caching is
          enabled.

Value:

     Long tibble with repeat, fold, optional candidate, stage, elapsed
     seconds, and execution status.

Examples:

     ## Not run:

     summarise_sjsdm_tuning_timings(list_sjsdm_tuning_prediction_cache)
     ## End(Not run)
