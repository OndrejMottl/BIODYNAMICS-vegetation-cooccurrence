Combine Selected sjSDM Fold Artifacts

Description:

     Combines, deterministically orders, and validates complete
     out-of-fold coverage across selected-candidate fold results.

Usage:

     combine_sjsdm_selected_fold_artifacts(
       list_fold_results = NULL,
       data_assignments = NULL,
       taxon_names = NULL
     )

Arguments:

list_fold_results: List of fold results from
          [run_sjsdm_selected_fold()] or the cached builder.

data_assignments: Location-level fold assignment table used to derive
          expected coverage.

taxon_names: Full ordered response-taxon names.

Value:

     Named list with combined predictions and fold diagnostics.
