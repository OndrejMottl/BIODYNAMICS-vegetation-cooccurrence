Diagnose Duplicate Modern Communities

Description:

     Returns modern sample records with identical sorted community
     composition.

Usage:

     diagnose_duplicate_communities(data_community = NULL)

Arguments:

data_community: A long-format community data frame with `dataset_name`,
          `sample_name`, `age`, `taxon`, and `pollen_count` columns.

Value:

     A tibble with one row per record involved in a duplicated
     community signature. Returns a zero-row tibble when no duplicate
     communities are detected.
