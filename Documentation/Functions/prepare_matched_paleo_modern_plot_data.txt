Prepare Matched Paleo-Modern Plot Data

Description:

     Filters the matched synthesis table to finite association
     estimates and joins coverage counts that make unmatched-unit
     exclusions explicit.

Usage:

     prepare_matched_paleo_modern_plot_data(
       data_comparison_unit,
       data_comparison_coverage
     )

Arguments:

data_comparison_unit: Matched paleo-modern synthesis unit table.

data_comparison_coverage: Synthesis coverage table.

Value:

     A tibble containing paired estimates, their difference, and
     matched and excluded-unit counts for the corresponding tier and
     resolution.
