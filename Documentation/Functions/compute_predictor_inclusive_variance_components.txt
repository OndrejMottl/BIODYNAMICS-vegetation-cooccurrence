Compute Predictor-Inclusive Variance Components

Description:

     Computes the full variance contribution associated with each
     predictor domain without dividing shared fractions among domains.

Usage:

     compute_predictor_inclusive_variance_components(data_source)

Arguments:

data_source: A non-empty data frame with columns `age`, `component`,
          and `R2_Nagelkerke`. Missing fraction labels are treated as
          zero, and negative R-squared values are clamped to zero.

Details:

     Each shared fraction contributes in full to every predictor domain
     that it contains. Percentages use the sum of the seven clamped
     variance fractions as their denominator, so percentages across
     domains may exceed 100 percent.

Value:

     A tibble with three rows per age and columns `age`, `component`,
     `R2_Nagelkerke_predictor_inclusive`, and
     `R2_Nagelkerke_predictor_inclusive_percentage`.

See Also:

     [compute_shapley_variance_components()]
