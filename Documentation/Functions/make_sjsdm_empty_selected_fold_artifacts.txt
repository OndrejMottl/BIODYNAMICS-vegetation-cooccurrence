Make Empty Selected sjSDM Fold Artifacts

Description:

     Creates schema-compatible empty selected-candidate OOF prediction
     and fold diagnostic artifacts for scientifically inapplicable
     models.

Usage:

     make_sjsdm_empty_selected_fold_artifacts()

Value:

     Named list with empty `data_predictions` and `data_diagnostics`
     tibbles matching [run_sjsdm_selected_candidate_folds()].

Examples:

     make_sjsdm_empty_selected_fold_artifacts()
