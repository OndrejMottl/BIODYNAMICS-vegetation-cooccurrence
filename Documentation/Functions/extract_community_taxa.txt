Extract Community Taxa

Description:

     Extracts unique taxon names from in-memory long community data.

Usage:

     extract_community_taxa(data_community = NULL)

Arguments:

data_community: A data frame containing a `taxon` column.

Details:

     Taxa retain their first-seen order. An empty community aborts
     because downstream classification and model fitting require at
     least one taxon.

Value:

     A character vector of unique taxon names present in the data.

Examples:

     data_community <-
       tibble::tibble(
         taxon = base::c("Pinus", "Betula", "Pinus")
       )

     extract_community_taxa(data_community = data_community)
