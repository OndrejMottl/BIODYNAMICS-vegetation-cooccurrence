Prepare Scientific Fold Content for Calibration Hashing

Description:

     Excludes only measured preparation runtimes from calibration
     signatures. Fold assignments, observations, predictors and spatial
     strategy provenance remain unchanged. This does not alter the
     folds used for fitting.

Usage:

     prepare_sjsdm_cv_calibration_hash_folds(list_prepared_folds)

Arguments:

list_prepared_folds: Cached records returned by
          [prepare_sjsdm_tuning_folds()].

Value:

     A copy of the fold records without operational runtime fields.

Examples:

     prepare_sjsdm_cv_calibration_hash_folds(list())
