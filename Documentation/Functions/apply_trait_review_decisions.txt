Apply Trait Review Decisions

Description:

     Applies approved, non-overlapping trait exclusion and scaling
     rules.

Usage:

     apply_trait_review_decisions(data_trait_records, data_trait_review_decisions)

Arguments:

data_trait_records: Current trait records for one review stage.

data_trait_review_decisions: Validated decisions from
          [validate_trait_review_decisions()].

Value:

     A named list containing `data_trait_records_corrected` and
     `data_correction_audit`.
