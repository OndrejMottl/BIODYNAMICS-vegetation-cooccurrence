Compute the Fast Spatial MEM Basis Engine Result

Description:

     Fixed-seed Nyström-strategy implementation for
     [compute_spatial_mev_basis()].

Usage:

     compute_fast_spatial_mev_basis(
       mat_coords,
       fast_eigenvectors,
       fast_seed,
       fast_function = NULL
     )

Arguments:

mat_coords: Numeric projected-coordinate matrix.

fast_eigenvectors: Positive integer controlling the low-rank basis.

fast_seed: Positive integer used locally for knot selection.

fast_function: Fast Moran-eigenvector construction function.

Value:

     Named list containing the complete MEV matrix, fast projection
     state, engine method, and projection method.
