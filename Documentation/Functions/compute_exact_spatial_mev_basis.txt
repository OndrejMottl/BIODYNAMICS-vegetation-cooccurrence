Compute the Exact Spatial MEM Basis Engine Result

Description:

     Internal exact-strategy helper for [compute_spatial_mev_basis()].

Usage:

     .compute_exact_spatial_mev_basis(mat_coords, exact_function)

Arguments:

mat_coords: Numeric projected-coordinate matrix.

exact_function: Exact Moran-eigenvector construction function.

Details:

     `sjSDM::generateSpatialEV()` drops matrix dimensions when exactly
     one positive eigenvector is available and then errors while naming
     columns. This helper detects only that error and repeats the same
     calculation with dimension-preserving subsetting.

Value:

     Named list containing the complete MEV matrix, empty fast-basis
     state, engine method, and projection method.
