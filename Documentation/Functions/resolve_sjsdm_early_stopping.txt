Resolve sjSDM Early-Stopping Patience

Description:

     Internal helper that preserves the three-tier early-stopping
     semantics used by `fit_jsdm_model()`.

Usage:

     .resolve_sjsdm_early_stopping(iter = 100L, n_early_stopping = NULL)

Arguments:

    iter: Positive number of fitting epochs.

n_early_stopping: Requested patience or `NULL` for automatic mode.

Value:

     Integer early-stopping patience passed to `sjSDMControl()`.
